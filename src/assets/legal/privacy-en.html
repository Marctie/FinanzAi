FinanzAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

FinanzAI ("the App") is a personal finance management application. This policy explains what data is processed and how, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play's data safety guidelines.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is the developer of FinanzAI. For any privacy-related request, please write to: finanzai.app@gmail.com (dedicated address — see note at the bottom of this page).

2. Data processed and where it is stored

All financial data you enter into the App — transactions, amounts, categories, notes, budgets, savings goals and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device (using the app's built-in local browser storage, "localStorage"). This data:

  • is never sent to any external server, neither the developer's nor any third party's;
  • is not automatically synced to the cloud;
  • remains accessible only from your device, until you delete it or uninstall the app.

3. Manual backup

The App offers a manual export feature that lets you save your data as a JSON (backup) or CSV file, which you can save or share yourself using your device's own tools (e.g. Files app, email, third-party cloud services of your choice). The developer has no access to these files: managing and sharing them is entirely under the user's control. The App does not integrate any automatic cloud backup service (e.g. Google Drive): uploading the backup file to a cloud service, if you choose to do so, is a manual, voluntary action performed outside the App.

4. Data collected by the developer

The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any personal or financial data entered into the App. The App does not include:

  • user accounts or login systems;
  • analytics/tracking tools that send data to external servers;
  • third-party advertising;
  • advertising or profiling SDKs.

5. Device permissions

The App may request access to device storage solely to allow saving/reading the backup file when the user chooses to export or import their data. No permission is used for any purpose other than the one stated.

6. "Automatic suggestions" feature

The App includes a category-suggestion feature and some statistical insights based on simple rules and mathematical calculations performed entirely on the device. This feature does not use any external artificial intelligence service and does not transmit any data to remote servers.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user manually deletes it from the App ("Delete all data" function) or uninstalls the application, which automatically removes all associated local data.

8. User rights

Since data is stored exclusively on the user's device, the user has full, direct control over it: they can view, modify, export, or delete it at any time using the App's features, without needing to request this from the developer.

9. Children

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children, as it does not collect any personal data in the first place.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated in the future, for example if new features involving data processing are added. The updated version will always be available at this same address, with the last update date indicated.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy: finanzai.app@gmail.com

Note: the email address shown on this page is a dedicated app address, separate from any personal contact of the developer.
